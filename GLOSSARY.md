# CastManager

CastManager lets a person play a video or audio file from their Android phone on a nearby TV or speaker that accepts Cast.

## Language

**Cast Device**:
Any device on the local network that can receive Cast, such as a Chromecast, a Google TV, a Nest Hub or a TV with Cast built in.
_Avoid_: Chromecast (except for that specific product), receiver, TV, target

**Media File**:
A single video or audio file chosen through the Android file picker.
_Avoid_: Media, video, content, stream

**Cast Session**:
The connection between the app and one Cast Device. It can exist with no Playback.
_Avoid_: Connection, pairing

**Playback**:
One Media File playing on the Cast Device of the current Cast Session. Only one Playback exists at a time.
_Avoid_: Stream, cast (as a noun), queue
